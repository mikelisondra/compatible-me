      HOW TO PLAY

      1. Create or Join: Enter your name. One person Hosts, others join using the Room Code.

      2. Choose Mode:
      • 1v1 Duel: Find out how compatible you are with a partner.
      • Party: Compete with friends on a leaderboard.

      3. The Game: The Host answers first (or sets the ranking). Guests must guess what the Host chose!

      4. Winning: The closer your guess is to the Host's answer, the higher your score!
